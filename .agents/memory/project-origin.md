name: Project origin
description: The user's firsthand motivation and the original procurement workflow problem.
---

The user's motivation comes from occasionally helping at their parents' SME automotive workshop. When a customer needed a rare-model spare part missing from inventory and the supplier catalog, their mother spent hours finding suppliers, confirming prices, placing orders, and recording everything in a separate Excel folder from the inventory system.

**Why:** This firsthand experience is the concrete user story behind the procurement MVP.

**How to apply:** Anchor design explanations and priorities in reducing fragmented supplier sourcing, quote confirmation, ordering handoffs, and recordkeeping. Do not present this personal experience as broader customer research.
