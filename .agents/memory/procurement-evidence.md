---
name: Procurement evidence
description: Trust boundaries for supplier recommendations and procurement automation.
---

Never let AI-generated supplier prices, review ratings or delivery promises appear as verified procurement evidence. Recommendations must be grounded in recorded quotes, with demo data identified explicitly.

**Why:** SME owners already make urgent purchasing decisions with incomplete information; unverified AI claims would recreate the problem this product is intended to solve.

**How to apply:** When adding live sourcing, preserve source URLs, observation times, currency and unknown fields. Separate draft communications from sent messages, and internal purchase records from supplier-confirmed orders. AI should explain available evidence and uncertainty, not hide missing data.
